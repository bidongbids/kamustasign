0LCSign · Translator: Fingerspelling redesign
HTML design reference, not production code. It uses the same 8 theme palettes as the classroom theming handoff, so it follows Light/Dark and the colour scheme. Desktop 1440×900, laptop 1366×768, phone 390×844. Red pins match the notes. See README.md beside this file.

F1Signing a word · Graphite Aurora · dark · desktop 1440×900
What's wrong today: the camera is the smallest thing on screen. Three equal columns compete. "No hand" is said three times. The letter, the confidence dial and the hold meter are three separate gauges for one moment. Tips take the space where the word should be. Engineering controls (commit speed, hold ms) sit in a permanent bottom bar, and there are two rows of tabs above all of it.
  1. Back is the same labelled pill as every other screen (it was a small icon).
  2. ASL | FSL is the top-level choice, centred in the header. The Signer button is removed from this screen. Speech language, Appearance and help sit on the right.
  3. Letters · Numbers · Words sit in one row underneath, so all three things you can translate are side by side. Words opens the word translator (record → stop → top matches) in the same frame.
  4. Signing hand stays visible because it affects accuracy, but as a compact toggle in that row. Commit speed moves into Settings (F3).
  5. The camera is the hero: ~60% of the width and full height, in a slim version of the material-pass housing (the shelves are gone). The status chip "Hand found / No hand" lives on the video, so it's said once. The guide box turns solid green when a hand is inside. FPS / model state shrink to a corner chip.
  6. Now signing merges three gauges into one: the recognised letter sits inside the hold ring (the ring is the hold progress, replacing the "Hold progress 0/450 ms" bar). Beside it: one sentence of what to do, and confidence as a bar + "92% · Sure".
  7. Your word gets the space: 56px Baloo with a caret, and the language it will be spoken in. The separate "Translation" column is removed, because the word is the translation for fingerspelling.
  8. Actions in two rows: Speak it aloud (accent, primary) + Show to someone, then Space · Delete letter · Clear word with text labels. Keyboard hints stay.
F2First use, no hand yet · Ocean · light
The three tips appear inside the empty word card, so there's no permanent tips panel. They disappear once the first letter is added and are always reachable from the ? button. Speak is disabled (45% opacity, with the hint "Spell a word first" for screen readers) until there's a word.
F3Settings popover open · Forest · dark
  1. Commit speed becomes "How long to hold a letter" with plain names and seconds (same three presets as today: Fast / Balanced / Careful). The value drives the hold ring.
  2. Signing hand is repeated here with its reason ("affects accuracy" becomes a sentence).
  3. "Hand points" turns the landmark overlay on or off, and "Speed (fps)" shows or hides the fps chip. Both are display-only, and Speed is off by default. The panel is an RN Modal popover on desktop and a bottom sheet on phone, and choices are saved on the device. The ? button opens the same three tips + keyboard keys in a similar popover.
F4States: Now signing (no hand · not sure · hold · added) · camera loading · camera blocked
The status sentences are today's strings, unchanged ("Show your hand to the camera." / "Not sure yet." / "Hold still… adding “B”." / the repeat hint). Each state differs by text and ring, not by colour alone: an empty ring with "?", a gold ring filling, then a full green ring and "Added". Ring fill is driven by the existing hold timer. With reduced motion it jumps in 25% steps instead of animating. Loading and blocked camera cover the video area only, so the rest of the screen stays in place.
F5Show to someone · Sunset · light
The existing full-screen view, now themed. It shows the word as large as fits, the translation if the speech language differs, and a Speak button. "Tap anywhere to close" still works, and Esc closes on web.
F6Laptop check 1366×768 · Graphite dark
At 768 tall: ring 118, word 44px, and the keyboard hint drops if space runs out. Nothing scrolls. Below 1100 wide (tablet) the layout switches to the phone stack with wider gutters.
F7Phone 390×844 · Graphite dark · Ocean light · Settings sheet (Forest light)
On a phone, ASL | FSL and then Letters · Numbers · Words sit at the top, above the full-width camera. Below the camera: a compact Now signing row (92px ring) and the word. A fixed bottom bar holds Speak (accent, full width), Show to someone, Space and Delete, all 52px. Clear word, signing hand and hold speed move into the Settings sheet. Portrait only; nobody holds a phone sideways to fingerspell.
