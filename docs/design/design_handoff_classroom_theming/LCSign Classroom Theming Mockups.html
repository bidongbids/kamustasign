0LCSign · Classroom home redesign, back button, light/dark mode and 4 colour schemes for every screen
HTML design reference, not production code. Each frame is drawn with one of 8 palettes (4 schemes × light/dark), applied through the same token names, so the same screen can be shown in any scheme. Desktop 1440×900, phone 390×844, laptop 1366×768. Red pins match the notes. See README.md beside this file for the token tables, the component change list and the copy.

HClassroom home (student), organised into three tabs
The classroom home now shows one thing at a time. A slim strip stays on top of every tab: hello + total signs learned, plus your teacher with a Call button, so calling is always one tap away. Under it, three tabs: Today (what to do next), My signs (the letter/word tiles) and From my teacher (modules + calling). The Back button, the Appearance panel and all 8 palettes are unchanged from the previous version.
H1Tab 1 · Today (opens by default) · Graphite Aurora · dark · desktop 1440×900
  1. Top strip (on every tab): "Hi, Ana!" + total signs learned + the overall bar (same computeOverall value).
  2. Your teacher pill: avatar, name, presence (dot + words) and a 44px Call button. It's always visible, so calling never depends on which tab is open. Busy: "Call (they're on a call)". No teacher yet: the pill reads "No teacher yet" and has no button.
  3. Page tabs (new PageTabs): three 48px pills with icon, label and count, role="tablist". Today opens by default. The last tab used is remembered on the device. Arrow keys move between tabs on web.
  4. Next up is the hero of Today: a 150px sign slab, one sentence of encouragement from real counts, and Practise V. "Pick another sign" jumps to the My signs tab.
  5. Due soon: the nearest module with a due date (or the first unfinished one if none has a due date). "Continue" practises its next unlearned sign. Hidden when there are no modules.
  6. How you're doing: one card per available group with "21 of 26 learned" and a bar. Tapping a card opens My signs on that group. Groups with no model yet sit together in one dashed "SOON" card instead of being repeated.
H2Tab 2 · My signs · Graphite Aurora · light
  1. The tile grid gets the whole tab. ASL|FSL and Letters / Numbers / Words stay as the existing SegmentedToggle + CurriculumTabs, with counts inside. They're second-level tabs inside the page tab, styled smaller and on the card so the two levels don't compete.
  2. Tiles keep the calm style: small check inside the corner, stars, a dashed outline for not yet, an accent ring + "NEXT" for the next sign. 13 per row on desktop.
  3. A one-line hint replaces the long rules paragraph. The legend is two items.
H3Tab 3 · From my teacher · Forest · dark
  1. One card per module, in the teacher's order: name, group + size, a due chip ("Due Sun, Nov 1 · in 5 days") or a Done chip, the module's signs as chips (learned ones get a check, the rest are dashed) and "1 of 7 learned".
  2. One action per module: Continue / Start (accent) practises the next unlearned sign in the module, and Practise again appears when it's done.
  3. Your teacher card: a bigger version of the strip's call pill with a one-line explanation, and the missed-call notice when there is one (existing missed-call data).
No modules yet: "Your teacher hasn't added any modules yet. You can still practise everything in My signs." with a button to that tab.

H6Phone 390×844 · the three tabs become a bottom tab bar · Today (Graphite dark) · My signs (Ocean light) · From my teacher (Forest light) · Appearance sheet (Sunset light)
On phones the same three tabs move to a bottom tab bar (112×50 targets, safe-area padded), the same pattern as the teacher's phone layout. The top strip shrinks to hello + total + a compact teacher pill with Call. Today stacks Next up, Due soon and the group bars. My signs shows the tiles at 58px, 5 per row (children's targets stay ≥ 48). From my teacher lists the modules with one full-width action each, with the missed-call notice on top.
BThe back button: one rule for every screen
Always top-left, always the same pill with an arrow and a word. It goes to the screen you came from in the app's own hierarchy, not the browser history, so it behaves the same in the desktop app, the web app and on phones. The browser and Android back buttons do the same thing.
B1Where Back goes (header strips, Graphite light)
RN: one BackButton component (label, onPress, accessibilityLabel = "Back to {destination}"). DashboardShell gets onBack + backLabel and wires the Android hardware back button (BackHandler) and the web popstate event to the same handler. Unsaved-changes guard: ConfirmInline in the sticky save bar, never window.confirm.
TATeacher and Admin dashboards with Back, Appearance and the schemes
Same layouts as the Teacher/Admin handoff, now drawn from the theme tokens, so they follow whatever the user picks. Role colours map onto the scheme: the teacher accent is the scheme's primary, the student accent is the scheme's accent, and admin stays neutral grey-blue in every scheme.
