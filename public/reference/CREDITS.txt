ASL letters  (public/reference/asl-letters/*.jpg)
  "ASL Alphabet" by Akash Nagaraj (2018), Kaggle.
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/grassknoted/asl-alphabet
  DOI: 10.34740/KAGGLE/DSV/29550
  License: GNU General Public License v2 - see asl-letters/LICENSE-GPL-2.0.txt
  Changes: one photo per letter selected from the training set; the 2px
  blue frame around each photo trimmed.

FSL words  (public/reference/fsl-words/*.mp4)
  "FSL-105: A dataset for recognizing 105 Filipino sign language videos"
  by Isaiah Jassen Tupal, De La Salle University (2023). Mendeley Data, V2.
  https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/48y2y99mb9/2
  DOI: 10.17632/48y2y99mb9.2
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
           - see fsl-words/LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt
  Changes: one clip per word (signer "D"), trimmed to the signing motion,
  cropped to 4:3, re-encoded to H.264 at 30 fps, audio removed.
  File names are the dataset's class folder numbers.

ASL words  (public/reference/asl-words/*.mp4)
  One clip per word, from:
  - "ASL Citizen" by Desai, Berger, Minakov et al., Microsoft Research (2023).
    https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/asl-citizen/
    License: Microsoft Research License Terms - non-commercial research use;
    does not allow distributing the data.
  - "WLASL" by Dongxu Li et al. (2020). https://dxli94.github.io/WLASL/
    License: Computational Use of Data Agreement (C-UDA).
  - "MS-ASL" by Hamid Vaezi Joze and Oscar Koller, Microsoft (2019).
    https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/ms-asl/
    License: Computational Use of Data Agreement (C-UDA).
  These licenses cover research and training use, not showing the footage.
  They are shown here only inside this non-commercial capstone prototype, by
  the project owner's decision; ask the dataset authors before any public
  release. The clip per word was chosen by
  ml/scripts/select_asl_word_references.py (the one the word model
  recognises best); the source of each is in src/data/referenceMedia.json.
  Changes: trimmed to the signing motion, scaled to 360px high, cropped to
  4:3, re-encoded to H.264 at 30 fps, audio removed.
